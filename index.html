The Quranic Human Ranks
دَرَجَاتُ الإِنْسَانِ فِي الْقُرْآن
Seven inner qualities the Quran distinguishes — not what someone does, but what someone has become. Each rank names a constitutive character trait: a permanent orientation of the soul toward Allah.
سات باطنی اوصاف جو قرآن نے الگ کیے — کوئی کیا کرتا ہے نہیں، کیا بن گیا ہے۔ ہر درجہ روح کا ایک مستقل رخ بیان کرتا ہے۔

The Seven Inner Qualities
Tab I
الْمُخْلِصُون
Al-Mukhlisoon
مخلص — خالص کرنے والے
Root: خ — ل — ص · To purify · To extract
Active vs Passive sincerity. The grammatical distinction between Mukhlisoon (those who purify) and Mukhlasoon (those purified by Allah). Iblis himself testifies to this rank in 38:82-83.

Tab II
الْأَوَّابُون
Al-Awwaaboon
اواب — مسلسل لوٹنے والے
Root: أ — و — ب · To return repeatedly
Habitual return to Allah — not the single act of tawbah but a permanent inner orientation. Dawud, Sulayman, and Ayyub AS — power, abundance, suffering — all praised with this one quality.
Open Tab II
Tab III
الصَّابِرُون
Al-Sabiroon
صابر — صبر کرنے والے
Root: ص — ب — ر · To restrain · To hold back
Three types the Quran distinguishes — all required. Sabr in obedience, Sabr from sin, Sabr under trial. The only quality with a limitless reward: bi-ghayri hisab (without account).
Open Tab III
Tab IV
الشَّاكِرُون
Al-Shakiroon
شاکر — شکر کرنے والے
Root: ش — ك — ر · To recognise · To give due thanks
"Few of My servants are grateful" — 34:13. Three levels: tongue, heart, and limbs. Shukr as the mirror and partner of Sabr — together they cover the full spectrum of human experience.
Open Tab IV
Tab V
الْقَانِتُون
Al-Qaanitoon
قانت — مستقل عبودیت والے
Root: ق — ن — ت · Sustained continuous devotion
Not an act of worship but a permanent inner posture. Maryam AS named Qanitah — the model. Qunoot = continuous orientation of the soul, not an occasional performance.
Open Tab V
Tab VI
أُولُو الْأَلْبَاب
Ulu al-Albab
اولو الالباب — باطنی مرکز والے
Root: ل — ب — ب · The innermost kernel · Pure essence
The four-layer inner architecture: Sadr, Qalb, Fu'ad, Lubb. The Quran was revealed for Ulu al-Albab to do Tadabbur. Defined by simultaneous Dhikr and Tafakkur — 16 occurrences, never negative.
Open Tab VI
Tab VII
الرَّاسِخُونَ فِي الْعِلْم
Al-Rasikhoon fil-Ilm
راسخون — علم میں گہری جڑیں
Root: ر — س — خ · To sink deep · To become firmly rooted
The rarest quality — appears only twice in the Quran. Knowledge that roots vs knowledge that moves. When the Mutashabihat come, they say: "We believe — all is from our Lord."
