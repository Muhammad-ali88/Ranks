The Seven Qualities — In Order
Each quality is a station the soul can build — not a gift reserved for the few, but the natural result of sustained orientation toward Allah. The Quran names each one as a present-tense description of people who exist in every generation.
ہر صفت ایک مقام ہے جو روح بنا سکتی ہے — چند کے لیے مخصوص تحفہ نہیں، بلکہ اللہ کی طرف مستقل رخ کا فطری نتیجہ۔ قرآن ہر ایک کو حال کی تفصیل کے طور پر بیان کرتا ہے۔
I
الْمُخْلِصُون
Al-Mukhlisoon
مخلصون — خالص کرنے والے
Root · خ ل ص · To purify
Active Sincerity
The grammar is the insight. Mukhlisoon (active — those who are purifying their intention) vs Mukhlasoon (passive — those purified by Allah). One is the sustained effort, the other is the divine response to that effort. Iblis himself testifies he has no power over the Mukhlasoon — which tells us precisely what this quality protects against.
إِلَّا عِبَادَكَ مِنْهُمُ الْمُخْلَصِينَ
"Except Your servants among them who are purified." — Iblis testifies to this rank
Surah Sad 38:83
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Muhasibi · Al-Ghazali

II
الْأَوَّابُون
Al-Awwaaboon
اوابون — مسلسل لوٹنے والے
Root · أ و ب · Habitual return
Constant Return
Not the single act of Tawbah — that is the minimum required of every believer. The Awwab is the person for whom returning to Allah after every drift has become constitutive of their character. Three prophets in one surah — Dawud with power, Sulayman with abundance, Ayyub with suffering — all praised with this one quality. Awwab transcends circumstance.
هَٰذَا مَا تُوعَدُونَ لِكُلِّ أَوَّابٍ حَفِيظٍ
"This is what you were promised — for every Awwab, Hafeez." — At the gates of Paradise
Surah Qaf 50:32
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali

III
الصَّابِرُون
Al-Sabiroon
صابرون — صبر کرنے والے
Root · ص ب ر · To restrain
Three Types · All Required
The Quran distinguishes three types — Sabr in obedience (sustaining worship), Sabr from sin (restraining the nafs), and Sabr under trial (holding through loss). All three required simultaneously. The only quality with a limitless reward: bi-ghayri hisab — without account. And the only quality that earns divine Ma'iyya: Allah is WITH the Sabiroon.
إِنَّ اللَّهَ مَعَ الصَّابِرِينَ
"Indeed Allah is WITH the Sabiroon." — The highest divine accompaniment named
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:153
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali

IV
الشَّاكِرُون
Al-Shakiroon
شاکرون — شکر کرنے والے
Root · ش ك ر · To recognise
Rare · Three Levels
Allah says: "Few of My servants are truly grateful" — making this one of the rarest qualities in the Quran. Three levels: Shukr of the tongue (Hamd), heart (recognising the gift is from Allah), and limbs (using the gift in obedience). Shukr is the mirror of Sabr — together they cover the full spectrum of human experience.
وَقَلِيلٌ مِّنْ عِبَادِيَ الشَّكُورُ
"And few of My servants are truly grateful." — Said in the context of Sulayman AS
Surah Saba 34:13
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali

V
الْقَانِتُون
Al-Qaanitoon
قانتون — مستقل عبودیت والے
Root · ق ن ت · Sustained devotion
Inner Posture · Not an Act
Qunoot is not a specific act of worship — it is a permanent inner posture of sustained devotion. Not an occasional performance but a continuous orientation of the soul. Maryam AS is named Qanitah — the Quran's clearest model: complete inner submission expressed in every state, independent of outward circumstance.
وَكَانَتْ مِنَ الْقَانِتِينَ
"And she was of the Qaaniteen." — Said of Maryam AS
Surah Al-Tahrim 66:12
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali

VI
أُولُو الْأَلْبَاب
Ulu al-Albab
اولو الالباب — باطنی مرکز والے
Root · ل ب ب · Innermost kernel
16 Occurrences · Never Negative
Lubb is the innermost layer — deeper than Sadr, Qalb, and Fu'ad. The Quran was revealed specifically for Ulu al-Albab to do Tadabbur. They are defined by simultaneous Dhikr and Tafakkur — remembering Allah in all positions while reflecting on creation. The Lubb cannot be acquired; it can only be uncovered by clearing what covers it.
الَّذِينَ يَذْكُرُونَ اللَّهَ قِيَامًا وَقُعُودًا وَعَلَىٰ جُنُوبِهِمْ وَيَتَفَكَّرُونَ
"Those who remember Allah standing, sitting, on their sides — and reflect upon creation."
Surah Al-Imran 3:191
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali

VII
الرَّاسِخُونَ فِي الْعِلْم
Al-Rasikhoon fil-Ilm
راسخون — علم میں گہری جڑیں
Root · ر س خ · To sink deep
Rarest · 2 Occurrences Only
Appears only twice in the entire Quran — one of the rarest qualities named. The distinction is not knowledgeable vs ignorant, but knowledge that moves (surface, informational) vs knowledge that roots (penetrating all layers until it becomes the ground you stand on). When the Mutashabihat arrive, the Rasikhoon say: We believe — all is from our Lord.
وَالرَّاسِخُونَ فِي الْعِلْمِ يَقُولُونَ آمَنَّا بِهِ كُلٌّ مِّنْ عِندِ رَبِّنَا
"And those firmly rooted in knowledge say: We believe — all is from our Lord."
Surah Al-Imran 3:7
Ibn al-Qayyim · Al-Ghazali
