What "Allah Loves" Actually Means«اللہ محبت کرتا ہے» کا اصل مطلب

The Quran uses "Inna Allaha yuhibbu" (Indeed Allah loves) for specific human types — not believers in general, but particular qualities cultivated to a degree where they become the person's dominant characteristic. These are the most intimate statements of divine regard in the entire Book. Understanding what each love statement means — theologically, linguistically, and practically — is among the most important studies in Quranic anthropology. قرآن «إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ» مخصوص انسانی اقسام کے لیے استعمال کرتا ہے — عام مومنوں کے لیے نہیں، بلکہ خاص اوصاف کے لیے جو شخص کی غالب خصوصیت بن گئے۔ یہ پوری کتاب میں الٰہی توجہ کے سب سے قریبی بیانات ہیں۔

◈ The Theological Weight of Divine Love
◈ الٰہی محبت کا الٰہیاتی وزن
Divine Mahabbah in Islamic theology is not an emotion in the human sense — it is an ontological orientation of Allah toward the person that produces real effects: mercy flows toward them, protection encompasses them, tawfiq opens for them, He mentions them to the angels, and believers' hearts incline toward them on earth. Ibn al-Qayyim dedicates an entire book (Tariq al-Hijratayn) to mapping these effects. The critical point: when the Quran says "Allah loves the Muhsineen," it describes a relationship of special divine attention with measurable effects — not merely admirable qualities to aspire to, but the specific qualities that open the channel of divine love.
الٰہی محبت اسلامی الٰہیات میں انسانی جذبہ نہیں — اللہ کی اس شخص کی طرف وجودیاتی توجہ جو حقیقی اثرات پیدا کرتی ہے: رحمت، حفاظت، توفیق، فرشتوں میں ذکر، مومنوں کے دلوں کا جھکاؤ۔ ابن القیم (طریق الہجرتین) نے ان اثرات کا نقشہ بنایا۔ اہم نکتہ: یہ درجات صرف قابلِ رشک نہیں — یہ وہ خاص اوصاف ہیں جو الٰہی محبت کا چینل کھولتے ہیں۔

Root: ح — س — ن · Beauty · Excellence · Ahsana = doing something with complete excellence and full presence
جڑ: ح — س — ن · خوبصورتی · بہترینی · احسن = مکمل حاضری کے ساتھ بہترین کرنا
The Muhsinoon receive the love statement three times — more than any other rank. Ihsan is defined by the Prophet ﷺ in the Hadith of Jibreel as the highest of the three levels: "That you worship Allah as though you see Him — and if you do not see Him, then He sees you." The Muhsin's defining quality is not what he does but the state in which he does it — continuous awareness of being seen by Allah.
محسنون کو قرآن میں تین بار محبت کا بیان ملتا ہے — کسی سے زیادہ۔ احسان حدیثِ جبریل میں تینوں سطحوں میں اعلیٰ: «اللہ کی عبادت جیسے اسے دیکھ رہے ہو۔» محسن کی خاصیت عمل نہیں — کیفیت ہے جس میں کرتا ہے۔

H-S-N is the root of beauty and excellence — Husn (beauty as quality), Hasana (a beautiful/good deed), Ihsan (doing something with fullest possible excellence — the causative-intensive form). The morphological form Ihsan is specifically the causative-intensive: not merely doing good, but doing it with complete presence and awareness. The Muhsin is one for whom this excellence has become habitual — not an occasional peak but a consistent mode of operating. The same root gives Husn al-khuluq (beautiful character) and Ihsan ila al-walidayn (excellent treatment of parents).

"And spend in the path of Allah — and do not cast yourselves into destruction. And do Ihsan — indeed Allah loves the Muhsineen."
«اللہ کی راہ میں خرچ کرو — اپنے آپ کو ہلاکت میں نہ ڈالو۔ اور احسان کرو — بے شک اللہ محسنوں سے محبت کرتا ہے۔»
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:195
The Command Sequence — Ihsan Placed After Spending
Context: wartime spending. The command sequence is revealing — spend → do not withhold to the point of self-harm → Wa-ahsinoo (do Ihsan). The love statement follows the Ihsan command, not the spending command. Ibn Kathir: Ihsan here means giving with complete excellence of character — not reluctantly, not minimally, but with the full beauty of the act. The critical grammatical detail: Wa-ahsinoo is imperative plural — "do Ihsan" as an active performed thing, not "be good people" as a static quality. Every act can be done with or without Ihsan. The Muhsin consistently brings Ihsan to every act until it becomes nature.
سیاق: جنگی خرچ۔ حکم: خرچ کرو → نہ روکو → واحسنوا۔ محبت کا بیان احسان کے حکم کے بعد — خرچ کے نہیں۔ ابن کثیر: احسان یہاں بہترین کردار کے ساتھ سخاوت۔ واحسنوا — فعل امر: «ابھی احسان کرو»، نہ «اچھے لوگ ہو»۔

Madani · Al-Imran 3:134 — The Muhsin's Character Portrait
مدنی · آل عمران ۳:۱۳۴ — محسن کی کردار کی تصویر
Rev. #89ترتیب #89 Al-Imran 3:133-134 الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي السَّرَّاءِ وَالضَّرَّاءِ وَالْكَاظِمِينَ الْغَيْظَ وَالْعَافِينَ عَنِ النَّاسِ ›
الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي السَّرَّاءِ وَالضَّرَّاءِ وَالْكَاظِمِينَ الْغَيْظَ وَالْعَافِينَ عَنِ النَّاسِ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِينَ
"Those who spend in ease and hardship, and who suppress their anger, and who pardon people — and Allah loves the Muhsineen."
«وہ جو آسانی اور تنگی میں خرچ کریں، غصہ پیئیں، لوگوں کو معاف کریں — اللہ محسنوں سے محبت کرتا ہے۔»
Surah Al-Imran 3:133-134
The Most Precise Quranic Portrait of the Muhsin — Three Qualities
(1) Yunfiqoona fi al-sarra'i wal-darra'i — spend in ease AND hardship. Not only when comfortable. The Ihsan of giving does not stop when conditions change. (2) Al-Kadhimeen al-ghayth — Kadhm = to press something back down after it has risen. The anger rose. They pressed it back. This is more honest and demanding than "not getting angry" — the Quran does not ask for absence of anger but superior management of anger that has already arisen. (3) Al-'Afeen 'an al-nas — those who pardon. Not suppress anger privately while holding a grudge — but actually pardon. Sequence: suppress → then go further and pardon. Then: the love statement. These three qualities in three hard conditions define the Muhsin more precisely than any general statement.
(۱) آسانی اور تنگی دونوں میں خرچ — احسان حالات سے مشروط نہیں۔ (۲) الکاظمین الغیظ — غصہ اُبھرا، واپس دبایا۔ قرآن غصہ نہ آنا نہیں مانگتا — اُبھرے کو سنبھالنا مانگتا ہے۔ (۳) العافین — واقعی معاف کرنا، دبا کر رکھنا نہیں۔ ترتیب: دباؤ → معاف کرو۔
Root Study — Kadhm
كَاظِمِين · K-Zh-M · To press back what has already risen
Kadhm — pressing back of something that has risen, like pressing air from a waterskin. The Kadhim al-ghayth does not prevent anger from rising — he presses it back after it has risen. This places the Muhsin's quality in the realm of the achievable: anger is human. What you do with it is the test of Ihsan. The person who says "I never get angry" has not understood this ayah. The person who says "I got angry and pressed it back" is what the Quran is describing.
کاظم — اُبھری چیز کو واپس دبانا۔ قرآن غصہ نہ آنے کا نہیں کہتا۔ «میں کبھی غصہ نہیں ہوتا» اس آیت کی سمجھ نہیں۔ «غصہ آیا اور دبایا» — قرآن یہی بیان کر رہا ہے۔
What This Ayah Uniquely Establishes
Ihsan is not primarily a spiritual state — it is specific character qualities demonstrated in specific hard conditions: difficulty (not just ease), in anger (not just peace), toward people who have wronged you (not just strangers). The Quran locates Ihsan precisely in the hardest interpersonal conditions. The Muhsin's awareness of being seen by Allah produces concrete results in specific difficult situations — not just in prayer.
احسان بنیادی طور پر روحانی کیفیت نہیں — مخصوص سخت حالات میں مخصوص کردار۔ قرآن احسان کو سخت ترین حالات میں رکھتا ہے۔ محسن کی آگاہی مخصوص مشکل حالات میں ٹھوس نتائج دیتی ہے — صرف نماز میں نہیں۔

Madani · Al-Maidah 5:13 — Third Statement · Ihsan After Betrayal

Surah Al-Maidah 5:13
The Hardest Context — After Definitive Betrayal
The most demanding context for the love statement. People who broke covenants, were cursed, had their hearts hardened — full justification for a different response. The command: Fa'fu 'anhum wa'sfah — two verbs. 'Afw = to pardon the offence. Safh = to turn away entirely, to overlook without holding it in memory. Then: Allah loves the Muhsineen. The love statement placed specifically after betrayal, after everything that would warrant anger. Ihsan in the hardest conditions earns the love — not Ihsan in comfort.
سب سے مشکل سیاق۔ جنہوں نے عہد توڑا، لعنت ملی — دو فعل: عفو (جرم معاف) اور صفح (مکمل اعراض، یاد میں بھی نہ رکھنا)۔ پھر محبت۔ خیانت کے بعد، غصے کے جواز کے بعد — یہاں احسان محبت کماتا ہے۔
What the Three Statements Together Establish
The three build a progressive picture: 2:195 — Ihsan in material giving. 3:134 — Ihsan in anger and pardon with ordinary people. 5:13 — Ihsan in pardon after definitive betrayal. Each harder than the last. The love is not for Ihsan in comfortable conditions — it is for Ihsan when it is hardest to produce. The Muhsin who earns all three is the one whose beautiful-doing survives the hardest tests.

◈ Two Levels of Ihsan — Mushahada and Muraqabah
◈ احسان کی دو سطحیں — مشاہدہ اور مراقبہ
The prophetic definition gives two levels. First: "as though you see Him" — Mushahada (direct witnessing). The awareness of Allah is so vivid it is as if sight is present. Second: "He sees you" — Muraqabah (awareness of being watched). Even without the vivid awareness, you act knowing He sees. Ibn al-Qayyim's critical distinction: Muraqabah is the maintenance of the knowledge that Allah sees; Mushahada is the direct experiential presence of that reality. Both produce Ihsan. The Mushahada-level Muhsin no longer needs to remind himself — the awareness is constant. The Muraqabah-level Muhsin actively maintains it. The entire spiritual path exists to move a person from Muraqabah toward Mushahada — from knowing He sees to living in that knowledge continuously.
دو سطحیں: مشاہدہ (براہ راست گواہی — آگاہی اتنی واضح جیسے نظر ہو) اور مراقبہ (دیکھے جانے کی آگاہی)۔ ابن القیم: دونوں احسان پیدا کرتے ہیں۔ پوری روحانی راہ: مراقبہ سے مشاہدے کی طرف۔

Al-Ghazali — Ihya Ulum al-Din
Ihsan as the Destination of the Entire Ihya — Uncovered, Not Achieved
احسان — پوری احیاء کی منزل — حاصل نہیں، ظاہر ہوتا ہے
›
"The entire structure of the Ihya has one destination: the state described as Ihsan in the Hadith of Jibreel. All of the purification of blameworthy traits, all cultivation of praiseworthy ones, all dhikr and tafakkur — these are not ends. They are the clearing of obstructions. The obstruction between the believer and the state of Ihsan is the nafs with its diseases. When the diseases are treated, when attachments are loosened, when preoccupations are silenced — what remains is the heart in its natural state, which is the state of Mushahada. Ihsan is not achieved. It is uncovered."
«احیاء کی پوری ساخت کی ایک منزل ہے: احسان کی کیفیت۔ تمام ذموم کی صفائی، تمام محمود کی پرورش — یہ منزلیں نہیں — رکاوٹیں ہٹانا ہے۔ احسان حاصل نہیں کیا جاتا — ظاہر ہوتا ہے۔»
Al-Ghazali's most important claim: Ihsan is the natural state of the heart that has been uncovered, not a foreign achievement built from scratch. The fitrah already carries the capacity for Mushahada. What blocks it are the accumulated diseases of the nafs. The Ihya's four quarters trace this excavation: establishing outer practice (Islam) → clearing social diseases → removing interior diseases of pride, envy, self-deception → cultivating saving qualities of tawbah, sabr, mahabbah, tawakkul. When this programme completes, Ihsan emerges — not as a new addition but as the original state restored.

◈ Where the Four Scholars Diverge

Al-Ghazali and Ibn al-Qayyim treat Ihsan as trainable through structured programme — optimistic systematisers. Al-Muhasibi interrogates the sincerity of the effort — the diagnostician preventing counterfeit. Ibn Ata'illah describes the destination from the inside — for those already approaching it. Not hudan lil-nas (guidance for people). Not hudan lil-mu'mineen (for believers). Guidance for the Muttaqeen specifically. Al-Qurtubi resolves the apparent paradox: Taqwa is a spectrum, not binary. The Penetration Metaphor — Guidance vs Information
هُدًى · H-D-Y · Active guidance — leads to the right path

The same person reading without Taqwa receives information. This is the most practical implication of this ayah.

Context: after Uhud, a defeat partly caused by fighters abandoning their positions. The command sequence before the Tawakkul is everything: (1) Be gentle — not harsh leadership. Allah commands both Birr (righteousness — the same word used for treating parents excellently) AND Qist toward non-hostile non-Muslims — then gives the love statement. Ibn al-Qayyim — Ahkam Ahl al-Dhimmah
Justice as a Divine Name Reflected — Al-Muqsit in the Human
الٰہی نام کی عکاسی — انسان میں المقسط
›
"Justice toward non-Muslims in Islamic law is not a political concession — it is a religious obligation that carries the reward of divine love. The Quran gave the Muqsit love statement specifically in the context of dealings with non-Muslims for a reason: to establish that the quality of equity cannot be selective. A person who is just to fellow Muslims but unjust toward others has not achieved Qist — he has achieved tribal solidarity, which is a different and lesser thing. The Muqsit reflects Al-Muqsit — and Al-Muqsit's justice is universal."
«اسلامی قانون میں غیر مسلموں کے ساتھ انصاف سیاسی رعایت نہیں — الٰہی محبت کا اجر رکھنے والی دینی ذمہ داری۔ قسط منتخب نہیں ہو سکتی۔ مسلمانوں کے ساتھ انصاف لیکن دوسروں کے ساتھ ناانصاف: قسط نہیں، قبائلی یکجہتی — مختلف اور کمتر چیز۔»
Ibn al-Qayyim's Ahkam Ahl al-Dhimmah is the most systematic classical treatment of justice toward non-Muslims as a religious — not merely political — obligation. His argument: because Al-Muqsit is a divine name, the person who reflects this attribute must reflect it universally. A selective Qist does not reflect the divine attribute — it reflects human preference. The love reward is specifically for the universal reflection of Al-Muqsit, not for the selective application of equity.
ابن القیم: المقسط الٰہی نام ہے لہذا انسان کو آفاقی طور پر عکاسی کرنی ہے۔ منتخب قسط الٰہی صفت نہیں — انسانی ترجیح۔ محبت کا اجر آفاقی عکاسی کے لیے ہے۔

غ
Al-Ghazali — Ihya, Book on Inner Qualities
Justice Begins With Oneself — Inner Equity Before Outer
انصاف خود سے شروع — باطنی مساوات پہلے
›
"Justice toward others is impossible for a person who has not first been just toward himself — giving the soul its right (worship, purification), the body its right (lawful sustenance, rest), and the mind its right (reflection, learning), without transgressing any of them in favour of the others. The Muqsit's equity begins as an interior quality — each faculty given its due — and only from that foundation can it extend genuinely to others. A person unjust to his own soul cannot be a genuine Muqsit toward others."
«دوسروں کے ساتھ انصاف اس شخص کے لیے ناممکن جس نے پہلے اپنے ساتھ انصاف نہ کیا — روح، جسم، عقل ہر ایک کا حق۔ مقسط کی مساوات باطنی خاصیت کے طور پر شروع ہوتی ہے۔ اپنی روح کے ساتھ ناانصاف شخص دوسروں کے ساتھ حقیقی مقسط نہیں بن سکتا۔»
Al-Ghazali connects the Muqsit rank directly to the inner architecture: giving each faculty of the self its due — soul, body, mind — is the prerequisite for genuine outer equity. The person who starves their soul (neglects worship) or abuses their body (neglects the body's rights) has not achieved interior Qist, from which exterior Qist flows. His framework: inner equity → outer equity, not the reverse. The inner architecture (Sadr, Qalb, Fu'ad, Lubb each given its due) is the foundation of the Muqsit's universal justice.
الغزالی مقسط درجے کو باطنی معمار سے جوڑتا ہے: روح، جسم، عقل ہر ایک کا حق — بیرونی قسط کی شرط۔ باطنی مساوات → بیرونی مساوات۔

The relationship goes both ways: Allah is close to the Wali and the Wali maintains closeness to Allah. This reciprocity is the theological heart of the rank — it is not one-directional devotion but a genuine relational proximity sustained from both sides.

Surah Yunus 10:62-64
The Quranic Definition — Iman and Taqwa. That Is All.
The only place in the Quran where the Awliya Allah are explicitly defined. The definition: Al-ladhina aamanu wa-kaanu yattaqoon — those who believed AND used to have Taqwa (ongoing continuous form — maintained as a practice, not a one-time act). No miracles. No special lineage. No certification by a shaykh. No asceticism. No isolation. Iman + ongoing Taqwa. Ibn al-Qayyim's most important corrective: any person who has genuine Iman and maintains genuine Taqwa is among the Awliya Allah — regardless of social station, knowledge level, or any outward sign of spiritual achievement. The rank is accessible to any believer who fulfils its two conditions.
واحد جگہ جہاں اولیاء اللہ صراحتاً تعریف شدہ۔ تعریف: ایمان + مستقل تقویٰ (مسلسل شکل)۔ معجزات، خاص نسب، شیخ کی اجازت، زہد، تنہائی — کچھ بھی نہیں۔ ابن القیم: حقیقی ایمان اور حقیقی تقویٰ والا کوئی بھی اولیاء اللہ میں — چاہے جو سماجی مقام ہو۔
The Promise — No Fear · No Grief · The Complete Spectrum of Human Suffering Removed
لَا خَوْفٌ · لَا يَحْزَنُون · Future anxiety removed · Past grief removed · Both dimensions
La khawfun 'alayhim: no fear — khawf is anxiety about the future, about what is coming. La hum yahzanoon: they shall not grieve — huzn is grief about what has passed, sadness about the present. Together: the entire spectrum of human psychological suffering. Future-fear AND past-grief: both removed. This is not a promise of external ease — the Wali may face hardship. It is a promise of internal freedom: closeness to Allah produces a condition where neither future anxiety nor past grief has hold. Ibn al-Qayyim calls this the most complete description of inner peace in the entire Quran.
لا خوف: مستقبل کی فکر ختم۔ لا یحزنون: ماضی کا غم ختم۔ مل کر انسانی نفسیاتی تکلیف کا پورا طیف۔ یہ بیرونی آسانی کا وعدہ نہیں — اندرونی آزادی۔ ابن القیم: پورے قرآن میں اندرونی سکون کا سب سے مکمل بیان۔
What the Bushra in This Life Actually Is
Lahum al-bushra fi al-hayat al-dunya — for them is good news in this world. Ibn Kathir: the true dream (al-ru'ya al-sadiqah). Ibn al-Qayyim gives a broader reading: the bushra of this life is the sustained experience of closeness to Allah — the opening of the heart, tuma'ninah (13:28), and the love others naturally feel toward them not because they seek it but because Allah places it in hearts. The bushra is not an external event — it is the quality of inner life that Walaya produces from the inside.
ابن کثیر: سچا خواب۔ ابن القیم: اللہ سے قربت کا مستقل احساس، دل کا کھلنا، طمانینہ، اور وہ محبت جو اللہ لوگوں کے دلوں میں رکھتا ہے۔ بشری بیرونی واقعہ نہیں — ولایت باطن سے جو زندگی کا معیار بناتی ہے۔

Allah as Wali — The Reciprocal Movement

This is the inner architecture reaching its ultimate functional state — the Sadr expanded, the Qalb settled, the Fu'ad witnessing clearly, the Lubb fully operational.

Ibn al-Qayyim — Al-Furqan bayn Awliya al-Rahman wa Awliya al-Shaytan
The Most Important Corrective — Who the Awliya Are NOT
سب سے اہم تصحیح — اولیاء کون نہیں ہیں
›
"The Wali of Allah is not known by miracles, by a tomb, by a lineage, by the wearing of a particular garment, or by the claim of any person about themselves or another. The Wali is known by the two conditions the Quran gave: Iman and Taqwa — both genuine, both sustained. A person with great miracles attributed to him but without genuine Iman and Taqwa is not a Wali of Allah — he may be a Wali of Shaytan. And a person with no miracles, no fame, no social recognition — who has genuine Iman and maintains genuine Taqwa — is among the Awliya Allah, whether anyone knows it or not."
«اللہ کا ولی معجزات، مزار، نسب، خاص لباس، یا کسی کے دعوے سے نہیں پہچانا جاتا۔ ولی دو شرائط سے پہچانا جاتا ہے جو قرآن نے دیں: ایمان اور تقویٰ — دونوں حقیقی، دونوں مستقل۔ بڑے معجزات والا لیکن ایمان اور تقویٰ کے بغیر اولیاء اللہ نہیں — شیطان کا ولی ہو سکتا ہے۔ بے نام، بے شہرت، کوئی معجزہ نہیں — حقیقی ایمان اور تقویٰ والا اولیاء اللہ میں ہے۔»
Ibn al-Qayyim dedicated an entire book (Al-Furqan bayn Awliya al-Rahman wa Awliya al-Shaytan — The Distinction Between the Friends of the Merciful and the Friends of Shaytan) to this exact corrective. His argument is methodologically precise: the only valid criterion for Walaya is the Quranic criterion. Any other criterion — miracles, tombs, lineage, dress, popular acclaim — is not Quranic and cannot be used to determine the rank. The most practically important implication: the Awliya rank is not a clerical status or a spiritual hierarchy requiring human authorisation. It is a personal condition between a person and Allah, determined by their Iman and Taqwa, invisible to most people, and available to anyone who fulfils the conditions.
ابن القیم نے پوری کتاب اس تصحیح کو دی۔ ولایت کا واحد درست معیار: قرآنی معیار۔ کوئی اور معیار — معجزات، مزار، نسب — قرآنی نہیں۔ اہم نکتہ: ولایت پادری مقام یا روحانی درجہ نہیں — شخص اور اللہ کے درمیان ذاتی حالت، ایمان اور تقویٰ سے طے، اکثر لوگوں کو نظر نہیں۔
Al-Furqan bayn Awliya al-Rahman wa Awliya al-Shaytan — The complete corrective on who the Awliya are and are not

Al-Ghazali — Ihya, Book of Mahabbah
The Inner Experience of Walaya — What No-Fear and No-Grief Actually Feel Like
ولایت کا باطنی تجربہ — لاخوف اور لاحزن کیسا لگتا ہے
›
"The no-fear and no-grief promised to the Awliya is not the absence of difficulty — it is the presence of something greater than difficulty. The Wali may lose what others lose, suffer what others suffer, face what others face. What differs is the inner relationship to those experiences. Fear requires: (1) something you want to avoid and (2) the possibility that you cannot avoid it. The Wali has so thoroughly placed his preferences in alignment with Allah's will that the first condition no longer applies — what Allah wills is what he wants. Grief requires: (1) something valuable that is lost and (2) the belief that its loss diminishes you. The Wali has so thoroughly recognised that nothing outside Allah is ultimately valuable that the second condition no longer applies."
«اولیاء کو وعدہ شدہ لاخوف اور لاحزن مشکلات کی غیر موجودگی نہیں — مشکل سے بڑی کسی چیز کی موجودگی۔ خوف کی دو شرائط: (۱) جو بچنا چاہتے ہو (۲) بچ نہ سکنے کا امکان۔ ولی نے ترجیحات اللہ کی مرضی سے ہم آہنگ کیں — پہلی شرط ختم۔ غم کی دو شرائط: (۱) گمشدہ قیمتی چیز (۲) یقین کہ نقصان آپ کو کم کرتا ہے۔ ولی نے پہچانا اللہ کے علاوہ کچھ بھی حتمی طور پر قیمتی نہیں — دوسری شرط ختم۔»
Al-Ghazali's analysis of the no-fear and no-grief promise is the most precise treatment in the classical literature. He does not describe it as the absence of hardship but as a specific inner reorientation that removes the conditions under which fear and grief are possible. His framework: fear requires wanting to avoid something + the possibility of not avoiding it. Tawakkul removes the second (outcome placed with Allah). Mahabbah and Rida (love and contentment with Allah's will) remove the first (alignment of preferences with Allah's will). The Wali who has genuine Tawakkul and genuine Mahabbah no longer has the conditions for fear or grief — not because his life is easy, but because his orientation has fundamentally changed.
الغزالی: لاخوف اور لاحزن مشکلات کی غیر موجودگی نہیں — باطنی تشکیل نو جو خوف اور غم کی شرائط ختم کرتی ہے۔ توکل دوسری شرط ختم کرتا ہے۔ محبت اور رضا پہلی شرط ختم کرتے ہیں۔ ولی کی زندگی آسان نہیں — رخ بدل گیا۔
Ihya Ulum al-Din — Book 36 (Mahabbah and Rida) — The inner reorientation that produces no-fear and no-grief

Ibn Ata'illah — Al-Hikam al-Ata'iyyah
Walaya as a State Discovered Not Achieved — The Paradox
ولایت حاصل نہیں — دریافت کی جاتی ہے
›
"Do not seek to be a Wali of Allah — seek Iman and Taqwa, which are what the Quran commanded, and let the Walaya be what Allah grants. The person who seeks Walaya directly has misunderstood the rank: Walaya is not an achievement, it is a recognition. It is what happens when Iman and Taqwa have been so thoroughly embodied that Allah's guardianship is the natural consequence. The person who is trying to become a Wali is still working from the self. The Wali arrived at his station by working from Allah — doing what He commanded and leaving the designation to Him."
«اولیاء اللہ بننے کی کوشش نہ کرو — ایمان اور تقویٰ کی کوشش کرو جو قرآن نے حکم دیا، اور ولایت وہ ہونے دو جو اللہ دے۔ ولایت کارنامہ نہیں — پہچان ہے۔ جو ولی بننے کی کوشش کر رہا ہے ابھی خود سے کام کر رہا ہے۔ ولی اللہ سے کام کر کے پہنچا — حکم مانا اور نام اللہ پر چھوڑ دیا۔»
Ibn Ata'illah's contribution to the understanding of Walaya is the same as his contribution to the understanding of every highest station: the paradox of achievement. The self that tries to become a Wali introduces the ego into the process — which is precisely what Walaya requires to be absent. His Hikam describe Walaya as what is uncovered when the conditions are met, not what is constructed through effort. This does not eliminate the human role — the conditions (Iman + Taqwa) must be actively maintained. But the Walaya itself is given, not taken. The distinction: the person's work is the conditions; Allah's work is the Walaya. Confusing the two — claiming Walaya as your achievement — is the surest sign of its absence.
ابن عطاء اللہ: کارنامے کا تضاد۔ ولی بننے کی کوشش کرنے والی ذات نفس لاتی ہے — جو ولایت میں غائب ہونی چاہیے۔ انسان کا کام: شرائط (ایمان + تقویٰ)؛ اللہ کا کام: ولایت۔ ولایت کو اپنا کارنامہ سمجھنا اس کی غیر موجودگی کی سب سے یقینی علامت۔
Al-Hikam al-Ata'iyyah — Aphorisms on the states of the heart and the paradox of spiritual achievement
◈ The Awliya and the Inner Architecture — The Final Connection
◈ اولیاء اور باطنی معمار — آخری تعلق
The Awliya rank is the point at which the inner architecture reaches its ultimate functional state. The Wali's Sadr is expanded for Islam (6:125). The Wali's Qalb is settled in tuma'ninah through dhikr (13:28). The Wali's Fu'ad witnesses directly without distortion (53:11). The Wali's Lubb is fully operational — receiving the Quran at its intended depth (38:29). The no-fear and no-grief are the result of all four layers functioning at full capacity simultaneously. Al-Ghazali's entire Ihya programme points to this condition as its destination: the purification and cultivation of every layer until the divine Walaya becomes the person's living reality — not as a belief but as an experienced state that produces the bushra of this world and the next.
